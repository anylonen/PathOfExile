# Toxic Rain Pathfinder

> Toxic Rain Pathfinder is a fast-paced, smooth mapping league starter that can clear all content in the game. You unlock your primary skills in the first act of the campaign and will continue using them for the lifespan of the character.

## Tips, regex etc.

- League start regex: g-g-b|g-[gb]-g|b-g-g|Runn|rint
- Mapping regex: "!gen|s rec|ask" "m q.*([7-9].|\d..)%"
